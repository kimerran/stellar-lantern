Why Lantern

Your keys. Your coins. An AI watching your back.

Everything a Stellar wallet should do — send, swap, earn, and cash out — plus a safety net between you and every signature. The risk verdict is rules and a public registry you can check; the AI explains it in words.

key

Non-custodial

Your private keys are encrypted on-device and never leave it. No accounts, no custodians — just you.

security

Scan every signature

Payments, trustlines, contract calls — each one is decoded, explained in plain language, and gated when it's high-risk.

campaign

Report once, protect everyone

On testnet, every review checks the counterparty against Lantern's on-chain blacklist registry, and one tap reports a scammer to it — so the next wallet is warned too. It's a live Soroban contract anyone can inspect: CBJWD6SA…G623F on testnet, WASM hash 40fd3771…5be23783.

code

Open-source scanner

The engine behind every review is @lantern/scanner — MIT-licensed and standalone, so any Stellar wallet, dApp or CLI can decode, explain and screen a transaction before it's signed.

published_with_changes

Checked again at send

Right before a transaction is submitted, Lantern simulates and screens it once more — so a counterparty reported while you were reviewing still stops you.

shield_person

Guardian recovery

Lose your seed, keep your funds. Appoint guardians and recover onto a new device with Stellar-native multisig — no 12 words to lose.

savings

Mini-app browser

Install the Beta

Lantern isn't in the app stores yet. On Android, it's a one-tap APK. On Chrome or Edge, load the extension in Developer Mode — about a minute either way. Both are published on every release, with SHA-256 checksums next to them.

android

On Android? Just the APK

Download lantern-<version>-testnet.apk on the phone, open it, and allow installs from this source if asked. That's it — the steps below are only for the desktop extension.

1

Download & unzip

Download lantern-extension-<version>.zip and unzip it to a folder you'll keep — Chrome loads it from there every time.

2

Open Chrome extensions

Go to chrome://extensions in your address bar.

3

Enable Developer Mode

Flip the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner.

4

Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked and select the folder you unzipped — the one with manifest.json directly inside it.

5

Pin & open

Pin Lantern to your toolbar, click the lantern, and create or import a wallet.
